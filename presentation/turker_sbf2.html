TYPE IA SUPERNOVA COSMOLOGY

Pantheon+ (2202.04077) total distance uncertainty: $$ \sigma_{\mu,i}^2 = \sigma_{\text{meas},i}^2 + \sigma_{\text{floor},i}^2 + \textcolor{red}{\sigma_{\text{lens},i}^2} +\sigma_{z,i}^2 + \sigma_{\text{vpec},i}^2 $$
Jönsson et al. (2010) $$ \sigma_{\text{lens},i} = 0.055 z_i $$
Statistical covariance matrix: $$ C_{\text{stat}}(i, j) = \begin{cases} \sigma_{\mu, i}^2 & i = j \\[0.4em] \sigma_{\text{floor}, i}^2 + \sigma_{\text{lens}, i}^2 + \sigma_{z, i}^2 + \sigma_{\text{vpec}, i}^2 & i \neq j \ \&\ \text{SN}_i = \text{SN}_j \end{cases} $$
Gaussian Likelihood: $$ -2 \ln (L)=\chi^2=\Delta \vec{D}^{\top}\, C^{-1}_{\mathrm{stat} + \mathrm{syst}}\, \Delta \vec{D} $$
PARAMETER INFERENCE

MCMC Likelihood Models

1. Gaussian (Fixed)

$$\mathcal{L}_i = \mathcal{N}(\Delta\mu_i;\; 0,\, \sigma_i^2)$$
$$\sigma_i^2 = \sigma_{\rm int}^2 + \sigma_{\rm meas, i}^2 + \sigma_{\rm lens, fid}^2(z_i)$$

2. Gaussian (ACE)

$$\mathcal{L}_i = \mathcal{N}(\Delta\mu_i;\; 0,\, \sigma_i^2(\theta))$$
$$\sigma_i^2(\theta) = \sigma_{\rm int}^2 + \sigma_{\rm meas, i}^2 + \sigma_{\rm lens}^2(z_i, \theta)$$

3. Edgeworth Expansion

$$p(\Delta\mu_i) \approx \mathcal{N}(\Delta\mu_i;\; 0,\, \sigma_i^2) \left[1 + \frac{\kappa_3}{6\sigma_i^3}H_3\left(\frac{\Delta\mu_i}{\sigma_i}\right) + \frac{\kappa_4}{24\sigma_i^4}H_4\left(\frac{\Delta\mu_i}{\sigma_i}\right)\right]$$
$$\sigma_i^2 = \sigma_{\rm int}^2 + \sigma_{\rm meas, i}^2 + \sigma_{\rm lens, fid}^2(z_i)$$

4. Full Convolution

$$\mathcal{L}_i = \int \mathcal{N}(\Delta\mu_i - \delta\mu;\; 0,\, \sigma_{\rm int}^2 + \sigma_{\rm meas,i}^2) \, p_{\rm ace}(\delta\mu \,|\, z_i, \theta) \, \mathrm{d}(\delta\mu)$$

The Gaussian Parameter-Dependent Covariance Bias

In a Gaussian likelihood where the variance changes with cosmology ( \(\sigma_{\text{tot}}^2 (\Omega_m,w)\) ), the log-likelihood is: $$ \ln L=- 0.5\sum\left[\frac{(r_i - \mu_{th}(z_i;\theta))^2}{\sigma_{\rm tot}^2}+\ln\left(\sigma_{\rm tot}^2 \right)\right] $$ where \(\mu_i\) is the theoretical value of the distance modulus predicted by the cosmological model, and \(\sigma_{\rm tot}\) is the total distance modulus uncertainty.
